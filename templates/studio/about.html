Understand your resume

Resume Analyzer reuses our deterministic skill extraction and matching engine. It automatically organizes detected resume information into a dashboard with a transparent completeness checklist. An optional job description compares supported skills and explains coverage.

Scores measure detected skill coverage. They do not assess your overall ability or predict hiring outcomes.

Put your text to work

Document Studio extracts digital PDF and DOCX text and uses local Tesseract OCR for printed scans. For handwriting, choose Gemini AI for multilingual cloud recognition, TrOCR Base for offline English, Tesseract as a local alternative, or compare the two local engines.

Handwriting remains best-effort and needs review. Checkpoint availability and document quality affect results.

Workspace files are processed in temporary storage and removed afterward. Resume analysis and local OCR stay on this computer. The workspaces do not add candidate, job, or matching records. Existing recruitment features remain available to authorized users.
